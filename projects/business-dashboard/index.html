How the numbers work

Every view uses the reporting period above, ending on 31 March 2026. Table searches and status filters narrow only their own lists and exports; they do not change the overview or analytics.

Revenue: paid orders that are not cancelled. Average order value: that revenue divided by the same eligible order count. Pending orders: fulfillment is pending. Open inquiries: new or in progress.

Refunded orders stay cancelled; unpaid orders cannot be marked completed. Status and priority edits update all related metrics. No real financial transaction occurs.

Start fresh

Reset restores all original records, clears local activity and filters, and returns the reporting period to 30 sample days. It affects only this demo’s storage key.
