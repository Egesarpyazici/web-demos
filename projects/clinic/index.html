02 / A DIFFERENT STARTING POINT

like a conversation.

These are the principles behind the Avela concept. A design for a more considered experience, not a claim about an operating clinic.

  1. 01

    Listen before anything else.

    Make room for questions, preferences, and the things that matter to each person.

  2. 02

    Make the next step clear.

    Use plain language and explain the process, so people know what to expect.

  3. 03

    Let people set the pace.

    Design welcoming spaces and give people time to consider their choices.

05 / A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straightforward answers.

Is Avela a real clinic?

No. Avela is a fictional clinic identity created for a developer portfolio. The website demonstrates design, accessibility, and multilingual interactions. It does not offer medical services.

Can I book an appointment here?

No appointment can be booked through this demo. The form prepares a readable inquiry summary on your device. Nothing is submitted to a clinic or stored on a server.

What happens to the information I enter?

Form details stay in this page’s memory until you leave or reload. Only your language preference is saved locally when browser storage is available. Copying puts the summary on your clipboard; opening WhatsApp shares the message with that external service. Please use sample details and do not enter sensitive medical information.

Can I choose a date and time?

You can select a preferred date from tomorrow through the next 90 days and one of the sample time windows. These choices demonstrate the interface; they are not live availability or confirmed appointments.

Why is WhatsApp unavailable?

The demo intentionally has no destination number. The site owner can configure a real number to enable click-to-chat. Until then, you can copy your summary without sending it anywhere.

What if I need urgent medical help?

This website is not a medical service and is not monitored. For urgent medical help, contact your local emergency services or an appropriate healthcare provider. Do not use this demo for medical requests.
