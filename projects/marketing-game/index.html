    THE PLEASURE IS IN THE DISCOVERY

    A little intuition.
    A second look.

    No countdown. No leaderboard. No rush. Just a small ritual of looking, remembering, and finding a connection.

    1. 01 / REVEAL

      Let curiosity lead.

      Turn two cards to discover the objects beneath. Each pair of selections counts as one attempt.

    2. 02 / RECOGNIZE

      Make a connection.

      Matching objects remain on display. Different objects turn back, inviting you to look again.
