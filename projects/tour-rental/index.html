07 / A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear horizons.

Is Orivela a real charter operator?

No. Orivela is a fictional brand for a developer portfolio. Every vessel, setting, route, and price is illustrative. No travel service or real availability is offered.

How are multi-day experiences priced?

Both the start and end dates count. A same-day experience is one billable day; consecutive dates are two. Each day repeats the chosen private outing and package option. These are day charters, not continuous overnight yacht rentals. The estimator shows the permitted date range and maximum duration.

What does the base price include?

The fictional base model covers a private vessel concept, skipper, and standard-route fuel for the stated daily hours and included guest allowance. Extra guests, upgraded options, and chosen extras are itemized. The calculator adds no other fee or tax; this is not a representation of a real operator’s tax treatment or inclusions.

Does preparing an inquiry reserve anything?

No. The inquiry is generated entirely in your browser. Nothing is submitted or booked. If a real WhatsApp destination is configured, you may open the external service with a prefilled message and decide whether to send it there.

What happens to the details I enter?

They remain in page memory and are not saved to browser storage or sent to a server. Reloading clears contact details. Copying writes the inquiry to your clipboard. Opening WhatsApp shares the prefilled message with that external service. Please use sample details.

What about weather, accessibility, and cancellations?

A real charter would require direct confirmation of weather plans, vessel access, safety arrangements, passenger requirements, and cancellation terms. This fictional site makes no guarantees about those services.
