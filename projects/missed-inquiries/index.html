THE MODEL, IN PLAIN SIGHT
Delayed conversion = P × RD

D = max(0, log₂(max(T, 5) / 5))

P
Your assumed conversion rate within 5 minutes.
R
The proportion retained with each doubling in delay.
T
Your average response time in minutes.
D
Doublings beyond 5 minutes, including fractional doublings.

For example, 40 minutes is three doublings from 5. At 80% retention, the baseline conversion is multiplied by 0.8³ = 0.512.

Revenue, rounding, and the preset assumptions

Revenue is monthly inquiries × conversion rate × average sale value. We round each revenue to cents, then subtract current from fast-response revenue. Annualization multiplies that monthly gap by 12, assuming unchanged inputs. Fractional conversions are expected values.

Conservative retains 90%, Moderate 80%, and Aggressive 60% per doubling. These presets change retention only. They are illustrative choices, not findings from research. At 100% retention, delay has no modeled effect.

Defaults: 240 inquiries, 40 minutes, 250 USD per sale, 25% baseline conversion, and 80% retention. Currency changes the unit and formatting only.
